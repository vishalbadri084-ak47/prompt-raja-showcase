# Prompt Raja

Turn an idea into a clear, ready-to-use prompt for ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI tool.

**Try it:** https://prompt-raja.vishalbadri084.workers.dev

## How it works

1. Choose the kind of prompt you need: write something, automate a task, chatbot / agent, improve a prompt, control the output, or learn the basics.
2. Describe what you want the AI to do (up to 500 characters).
3. Generate the prompt and copy it.

Works on phone and desktop.

## Good to know

- Free to use, with a small hourly limit on generations.
- Your text is sent to Google Gemini (free tier) to write the prompt. Don't enter personal or confidential information.
